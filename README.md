# GP. Jacket for Vest

A Tampermonkey userscript for [Vest](https://next.vestmarkets.com): a one-click order ticket with risk and reward dials you can also type into, limit orders you drag into place on the chart and arm, your daily loss floor on the chart, a desk for all your accounts (switch, copy, and close or cut any of them), GP. Mirror (lock a pair of markets such as NDX / SPX or BTC / ETH; MAIN accounts copy the lead, OPP accounts take the pair's other market the opposite way, with the lead's stop as their target and its target as their stop), a Claim all button on the Portfolio page that claims the profit from every funded account in one click, five chart colour sets (Vest, Paper, Terminal, Midnight, Compound) plus ones you build and save yourself (body, border and wick for each candle, and the canvas), and keyboard shortcuts you can switch on and set yourself.

> **Code GIGA at checkout.** GP. Jacket puts referral code **GIGA** on every Vest account you buy, in place of any other code. It is on by default. To turn it off, click **GIGA** next to **Online** at the bottom of Vest, or use the gear on the ticket (Settings) → **CODE GIGA AT CHECKOUT** → **off**.

Formerly GP. Entry. If you installed it before 23 November 2026, install it again from the link below once: it updates in place and keeps your settings, and from then on it updates itself from here.

## Install

1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) in your browser.
2. Open **[gp-jacket.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deciphe/GP.-Jacket/main/gp-jacket.user.js)** and press **Install**.
3. Reload Vest.

## Updates

Tampermonkey checks this repo and updates the script on its own. To pull an update right away: Tampermonkey → Dashboard → GP. Jacket for Vest → **Check for updates**.

# GP. Dashboard for Vest

Your whole Vest profile in one screen: every account, every trade, what you spent and what you got paid, plus affiliate earnings. Press the GP. button on Vest.

Install: open **[gp-dashboard.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deciphe/GP.-Jacket/main/gp-dashboard.user.js)** and press **Install**. It updates from this repo the same way.
